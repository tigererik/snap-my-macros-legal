Privacy Policy

Snap My Macros is designed to work without an account and to keep your app content under your control.

Effective: 13 September 2026 Provider and data controller: Erik Tiger, Sweden Contact: support.snapmymacros@gmail.com

In short: no advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no account and no camera-image uploads.

Information handled by the app

Snap My Macros stores saved products, barcodes, lists, selected weights, preferences, scan statistics, purchase entitlement and event progress on your device. This information is used only to provide the app's features. The developer does not operate a server that receives this app content.

Camera and barcode recognition

The app uses the camera only when you open the scanner. Barcode recognition is performed on the device. Snap My Macros does not save or upload camera frames.

Open Food Facts product lookups

When you scan or import a barcode, the app sends that barcode to Open Food Facts over an encrypted HTTPS connection to retrieve product and nutrition information. No app account, advertising identifier or scan history is attached to the request by Snap My Macros. As with any internet request, Open Food Facts and its infrastructure receive technical network information such as your IP address and may process it under their own policies.

Open Food Facts is an independent service. Learn more in the Open Food Facts terms of use.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current version of Snap My Macros does not send first-party analytics, advertising data, device identifiers, crash reports or histories of your interactions to the developer or an analytics provider. The app contains no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Backup and restore

On Apple devices, app data may be backed up automatically to your private iCloud database when iCloud is available. On Android, app data may be included in the device's system backup. These services are controlled through your Apple or Google account. Snap My Macros does not operate a separate backup server.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes in-app purchases and payment information. Snap My Macros receives purchase status and transaction verification information only to unlock Pro and restore a previous purchase. The developer does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it in the app or uninstall the app. Platform backups may remain according to your iCloud or Android backup settings and can be managed through the corresponding account. Open Food Facts, Apple and Google determine the retention of information they process through their own services.

Sharing and sale

The developer does not sell personal information and does not share information for advertising or tracking. A barcode and ordinary network information are processed by Open Food Facts only when a product lookup is requested. Apple or Google processes backup and purchase information to provide those platform services.

Security

Product lookups use encrypted HTTPS connections. Information kept by the app is protected by the security controls of your device and, where applicable, your Apple or Google account. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Nutrition information

Nutrition data is community-maintained and can be incomplete or incorrect. Snap My Macros is for information and comparison only and does not provide medical or dietary advice. Verify the physical product label and seek qualified professional advice when making health or dietary decisions.

Your choices and rights

You can delete locally stored products and lists in the app and manage platform backups through your device or account settings. Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights concerning personal information, including rights to information, access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and complaint to a supervisory authority. Because the developer does not receive your locally stored app content, the developer may not hold data that can be returned or deleted on your behalf.

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support.snapmymacros@gmail.com. In Sweden, you may also contact the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Children

Snap My Macros is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account or knowingly collect a child's name, contact details or precise location.

Changes
